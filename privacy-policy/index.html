Privacy Policy

How we handle information you share with us through this website.

Last updated October 2026

Information we collect

When you use the contact form or email us, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address, the service you are interested in, and your message. Our web host may also collect standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you visit, in server logs.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your inquiry, to arrange an assessment or services you request, and to keep a record of our communication with you. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing.

Health information

Please do not include detailed medical information in the website contact form. Once you become a patient or participant, health information is collected and protected under applicable federal and state law, including HIPAA, and is covered by the Notice of Privacy Practices we provide at the start of care.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising cookies. If we add analytics in the future, this policy will be updated to say what is collected and how to opt out.

Third-party links

Our resources may link to other websites, such as government health agencies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete the contact information you have shared through this website at any time by emailing office@discoveryhealthllc.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to office@discoveryhealthllc.com or by calling (267) 939-7727.
