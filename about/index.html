About Discovery Health LLC

Compassion. Quality. Independence. Always. We bring skilled nursing and everyday support to the place people know best: home.

Our mission

Health, independence, safety, and quality of life

Discovery Health LLC provides home health and community-based support services designed to promote health, independence, safety, and quality of life for individuals in their homes and communities.

That sentence is our mission, and it shapes every decision we make: who we hire, how we plan care, how carefully we document, and how often we pick up the phone to talk with a physician, a support coordinator, or a worried daughter three states away.

We believe that most people heal better, live better, and stay more themselves at home. Our job is to make home a safe place to do that.

What we value

The promises behind our care

Compassion

We treat every person as someone's parent, partner, or friend, because they are. Kindness is not an extra; it is how care works.

Quality

Licensed nurses, trained caregivers, careful documentation, and plans that are reviewed as needs change.

Independence

We help with what you cannot do and encourage what you can, so that staying at home stays possible.

Family partnership

Family members and caregivers are part of the team. We teach, we listen, and we keep everyone informed.

Coordination

One plan shared with your physicians, pharmacists, support coordinator, and the people who matter to you.

Community

Care does not stop at the front door. We connect people to the local resources that help them thrive.

How we work

Nurse-led, individualized, and documented

  • Assessment first. Care begins with a skilled nursing assessment of needs, risks, medications, and goals.
  • A plan you can read. Every individual has a written, individualized plan of care that is reviewed as their condition changes.
  • Monitoring and records. We watch for changes in condition, document what we see, and share it with the care team.
  • Education at every visit. Patients and caregivers learn how to prevent complications, stay safe at home, and manage chronic conditions.
  • Compliance built in. For CCC Plus participants, we provide ongoing guidance to help ensure compliance with applicable Medicaid and DMAS requirements.
